# Source transcriptions (fictional)

All people, places and letters below are **invented** for this project. Any
resemblance to real persons or places is accidental.

Each entry gives what an archivist would see: a catalogue reference, physical/
contextual notes, and a transcription. Square brackets `[ ]` are editorial
additions; `[?]` marks an uncertain reading.

---

## MAR/001

- **Dateline:** Hollinsford, 14th March 1821
- **Address panel:** Mr Thos. Marrable, Quay Street, Port Aldwick
- **Postmark:** HOLLINSFORD MR 14 1821

> My dear Brother,
> The thaw has come at last and the lane to the mill is a river. Mother bids me
> say the parcel arrived safe, though the jar of quince was broken in it.
> Write soon — your affectionate sister,
> Eliza Marrable

## MAR/003

## PEN/001

- **Dateline:** Wexcombe Vicarage, Saturday
- **Address panel:** Miss Hannah Greaves, Kelstow Hall, near Hollinsford
- **Endorsement (recipient's hand):** recd. 12 Aug. 1833
- **Archivist's note:** Written on or shortly before the Saturday preceding receipt.

> Dear Miss Greaves,
> I return your volume of ferns with thanks, and with a pressed specimen of my
> own which I believe to be new to the parish.
> Yours very sincerely,
> J. Penhallow

---

## PEN/002

- **Dateline:** Kelstow Hall, between 1832 and 1835 [catalogue date; letter undated]
- **Address panel:** The Revd. J. Penhallow, Wexcombe
- **Archivist's note:** Pre-dates the writer's marriage (1835); refers to the specimen in PEN/001.

> Dear Mr Penhallow,
> Your fern is certainly new to me, and I think to Mr Ashdown also, to whom I
> have taken the liberty of showing it.
> Hannah Greaves

## PEN/003

- **Dateline:** Wexcombe, spring 1843
- **Address panel:** Mrs Clara Ashdown, Hollinsford
- **Archivist's note:** Writer is the former Hannah Greaves (married J. Penhallow 1835).

> My dear Clara,
> The primroses are thick in the churchyard and Josiah has begun a list of them

## ASH/002

- **Address panel:** Mrs Ashdown, Hollinsford
- **Postmark:** PORT ALDWICK JY 3 1839
- **Archivist's note:** Dateline gives no date; postmark is the only evidence.

> My dearest Clara,
